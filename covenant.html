The Parampara Covenant
✦
Before you enter, please read and accept each commitment.
These six principles are the foundation of our shared heritage.
PRESERVING OUR HERITAGE
1.
Stewardship of Heritage — I enter this space with respect for Gurus, composers, practitioners, listeners, and the living ecosystem that sustains Carnatic heritage.
2.
Living Continuity — I understand that Parampara preserves evolving artistic lineages and that knowledge may deepen through collective contribution and verification.
3.
My Musical Journey — I am ready to share my musical history and lineage with the world. My name, artistic role, and teacher lineage may appear publicly to help map our shared heritage.
DATA & PRIVACY ASSURANCE
4.
My Personal Details — My personal contact details — including email, phone number, and address — remain private. My information will never be sold or shared with advertisers.
5.
My Consent — I confirm that I am 18 or older, or that a parent or guardian is assisting me in registration. If I leave Parampara, my profile may be removed while preserving lineage continuity for the record.
6.
My Data is Respected — My data will be used only for Parampara's mission of preserving and mapping Carnatic heritage, never for commercial gain or unrelated purposes.
